# Product steering — Pumpkin Patch

## What it is
A habit garden, not a checklist. Each habit is a plant in a haunted garden. Daily check-ins grow it; missed days wilt it; two missed days kill it and it joins the Graveyard with an epitaph. Harvest at 7 days for a pumpkin.

## Who it is for
Anyone who has quit a habit tracker because it was boring. The garden makes consistency visible and loss feel real — but playful, never punishing.

## Tone
Spooky but warm. Playful gothic: pumpkins, ghosts, moonlight, fog. No horror, no gore. Copy is short, witty, encouraging. The ghost is a friend, not a jumpscare.

## Principles
- One screen, everything visible. No navigation, no accounts, no settings maze.
- Every action gives immediate visual feedback (growth animation, ghost reaction, moon shift).
- Losing a plant must sting a little (tombstone + epitaph) but reviving must be one click.
- Works offline, loads instantly, no tracking.
